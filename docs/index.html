Questions

The short answers.

Is anything public?

No. Nothing is posted to YouTube or anywhere else. Only the people you send to can see your reactions.

Do my friends need an account?

No. You send them a link; they add the extension. A Google account is optional, never required.

Does this replace YouTube?

The videos stay on YouTube. What changes is who decides what you watch next, and who you talk to about it.

Your circle is the algorithm.
